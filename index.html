Scott N. Carter

Scott Carter has practiced exclusively in trusts and estates for more than thirty years and has worked with thousands of clients, including individuals, business owners, farmers, and executives. His planning ranges from simple wills to GRATs, charitable remainder trusts, QPRTs, dynasty trusts, and family business transfers, supported by estate and gift tax analysis.

He also handles trust administration, post-mortem planning, complex probate and trust litigation, and conservatorships, including contested estate planning and conservatorship matters. Theresa A. McGuire

Theresa McGuire works with individuals and families on estate planning and on reducing gift, estate, and generation-skipping transfer taxes. Her planning includes revocable living trusts, irrevocable trusts, special needs trusts, pet trusts, QPRTs, life insurance trusts, dynasty trusts, intentionally defective grantor trusts, GRATs, and charitable remainder trusts.

She represents individual and corporate fiduciaries in estate administration, including complex trust and probate administrations, and represents trustees and beneficiaries in the administration, amendment, and termination of trusts. She also advises on incapacity planning, equity sharing agreements, Medi-Cal issues, adoptions, and conservatorships. She was previously a senior associate in the Estates, Trusts & Tax Planning Department at Pillsbury Winthrop Shaw Pittman.

Practice Areas

Practice Areas

The firm concentrates in estate planning and administration, with related business and tax counsel.

Estate Planning

The firm prepares estate plans that govern the disposition and management of property and provide for decision-making in the event of incapacity. Engagements range from wills and revocable trusts to advanced planning for taxable estates. The firm also reviews and amends existing plans.

Covers: living trusts and wills, irrevocable and insurance trusts, special needs trusts, powers of attorney and healthcare directives, gift planning, QPRTs, charitable trusts, and trust amendments.

Trust Administration & Probate

The firm represents successor trustees, executors, and beneficiaries in the administration of trusts and estates, including fiduciary duties, notices, accountings, asset transfers, and distributions. The firm also represents parties in trust and estate disputes.

Covers: advising successor trustees and executors, trust asset division and transfers, probate, and estate and trust disputes.

Business & Succession Planning

The firm advises owners of closely held businesses on entity selection and formation, agreements among owners, and the sale or succession of the business, coordinated with the owners’ estate plans.

Covers: formation of corporations, LLCs, and partnerships; buy-sell agreements; business sales; and family business succession.

Tax Planning

The firm advises on federal estate and gift tax, California property tax, and income tax matters arising in estate and business planning, and represents clients before the Internal Revenue Service and the California Franchise Tax Board.

Covers: estate tax reduction, 1031 exchanges, property tax planning, representation before the IRS and FTB, and formation of tax-exempt organizations.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Carter & McGuire collects through this website and how we use it. If you have questions, contact us using the information at the end of this page.

Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, the type of matter, and any brief description you provide. We do not collect information from you in any other way through this website.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to begin our work with you. We keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or as required by law and our professional obligations.

We do not sell or share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing. We may use service providers, such as our email or website host, who handle information on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for any other purpose. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use cookies, analytics, or tracking tools. The embedded map on our Contact page is provided by Google, and Google may collect information under its own privacy policy when the map loads. If we add cookies or analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

Your California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, may give you rights over your personal information, to the extent the law applies to us. These can include the right to know what personal information we have about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the law. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. To make a request, contact us as described below. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

Security
