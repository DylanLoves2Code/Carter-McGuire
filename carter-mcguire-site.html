Practice Areas

What we do

Our work centers on estate planning. We also handle the business and tax matters that often go with it, so your plan fits together.

Estate Planning

A good estate plan says who receives your property, who manages it, and who makes decisions for you if you cannot. We build plans that fit your family and your assets, from a simple will to multi-trust planning for larger estates. We review existing plans and update them as your life changes.

Covers: living trusts and wills, irrevocable and insurance trusts, special needs trusts, powers of attorney and healthcare directives, gift planning, QPRTs, charitable trusts, and trust amendments.

Trust Administration & Probate

After a death, a successor trustee or executor has legal duties and many practical tasks. We explain each step, help you meet deadlines, and work to settle the estate efficiently. When family members disagree, we help resolve the dispute.

Covers: advising successor trustees and executors, trust asset division and transfers, probate, and estate and trust disputes.

Business & Succession Planning

For many clients, a business is the largest asset in the estate. We help set up the right entity, document the agreements among owners, and plan for the day the business is sold or passed to the next generation.

Covers: formation of corporations, LLCs, and partnerships; buy-sell agreements; business sales; and family business succession.

Tax Planning

Taxes affect how much of your estate reaches the people you care about. We plan to reduce estate and property taxes where the law allows, and we represent clients before the IRS and the California Franchise Tax Board.

Covers: estate tax reduction, 1031 exchanges, property tax planning, representation before the IRS and FTB, and formation of tax-exempt organizations.
